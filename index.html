FAQ

Questions You Might Have

How long does it take to build my website?

Around 2 weeks depending on the complexity of the project. Simpler landing pages can be done faster. I'll give you a clear timeline at the start.

Is the domain included in the price?

No — domain cost depends on your choice (e.g., .com, .in, .co.in) and is billed separately. I'll guide you on picking the right domain for your business.

Do you provide revisions?

Yes! Limited revisions are included in every package. You'll get a chance to review and request changes before the final delivery.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Absolutely. You can start with a Basic site and upgrade to Premium or Advanced anytime. Just reach out and we'll discuss the upgrade cost.

Do I need to provide content and images?

Yes — you'll need to provide your business content (text, logo, images). I can guide you on what's needed and help you structure it properly for the web.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

100%. Every website I build is fully responsive and tested on mobile, tablet, and desktop. Mobile-first design is a core part of my process.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Post-delivery changes are available at a small additional charge depending on the scope. I'm always reachable on WhatsApp for quick support.

Can you add a booking system to any package?

Appointment booking is included in the Advanced package. If you need it added to a Basic or Premium site, we can discuss a custom quote.
